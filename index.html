The Solution

The loop that closes the waste gap.

No extra steps for the cashier or the customer — the loop runs on data retailers already generate.

RECEIVE PURCHASE DATA
Ingested directly from the supermarket's POS system — no new hardware, no extra steps at checkout.
→
2
IDENTIFY & CALCULATE EXPIRY

→
3
SEND REMINDERS & REWARDS
Customers get timely, personalised reminders before products expire, paired with loyalty incentives to repurchase.
→
4
DELIVER RETAILER ANALYTICS
Inventory, customer, and operational intelligence surface on the retailer dashboard in real time.

"What did the customer buy?" is the wrong question. ExpirySmart asks: "What will they need to buy again, and when?"

Standard loyalty and CRM systems stop at the transaction. ExpirySmart closes the gap between inventory systems (what's in stock) and loyalty systems (what was bought) — a structural blind spot nobody else is covering.
